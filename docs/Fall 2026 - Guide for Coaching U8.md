## **Guide for Coaching U8 at AYSO Region 630**

**Thank you to all coaches, both new and returning\!**  
AYSO is 100% volunteer-driven, and the kids depend on you to play and learn. Let’s focus on helping them get as many gamelike touches as possible during practices. And remember to avoid the **3 Ls**: **Lines, Lectures, and Laps.**

---

### **Key Coaching Reminders**

1. **Communicate Often with Parents/Guardians**  
   * Regular updates build trust and ensure everyone is on the same page.

2. **Hold a Pre-Season Parent Meeting**  
   * Set expectations for behavior and outline the season's goals.  
   * Emphasize that **this is for the kids** and we’re all volunteers.  
   * Share the [**Coach-Spectator Behavior Guidelines**](https://www.ayso630.org/Default.aspx?tabid=971417).

3. **Remind parents to:**  
   * Cheer for their kids and let **you** handle the coaching.  
   * Pick a spot on the sideline during games and stay there (we understand they may need to follow younger siblings but running up and down the field isn’t helpful).  
   * Stay at least **1 yard back from the sideline** for safety, so kids have room to play.

---

### **U8 Rules and Best Practices**

#### **Equipment and Safety**

* **Shin guards** (under socks) are required for all practices and games.  
* Cleats are highly recommended.  
* **Ball size:** 3\.  
* No **jewelry, watches, or accessories** during practices or games. These must be removed (not taped over).

#### **Game Format**

* **Game start:** Games are to start at the scheduled time. Please have your players warm up prior to the game start time.  
* **Game length:** Two 20-minute halves with a substitution break mid-half (2-3 minutes).  
  * Halftime: 5 minutes (can be extended to 10 minutes on hot days for safety).  
* **Game size:** 6v6, with goalies   
* Goal Size: 12’x6’  
* Field Size: 50’x40’  
* **Substitutions:** 

  * Every player must play at least 3 quarters before anyone plays 4 quarters. With 8 players on a team, one player may sit out 2 quarters per game. Rotate this responsibility, making substitutions only at designated breaks/quarters, except for safety or injury. 

  * Players are only allowed to play goalkeeper 1 quarter per game. Substitutions for safety or injury can happen anytime. If a player is substituted for safety, they may re-enter during the same quarter at a stoppage of play, replacing the player who took their place.

#### **Key Rules**

* Goalkeeper  
  * When the goalkeeper retrieves the ball from an inbounds field play, the goalkeeper is only allowed to throw, roll or pass(on the ground) the ball to a player. Goalkeeper Punts are **not allowed.**  
  * Goalkeepers are not allowed to pick the ball up again after placing it on the ground from live play.  
  * Goalkeepers are only allowed to touch the ball with their hands within the designated goalkeeper box.  
* **Throw-ins**  
  * Allow play to continue after a bad throw-in but coach them on the proper technique:  
    * Two hands on the ball.  
    * Both feet on the ground, on or behind the sideline.  
    * Ball thrown overhead.

* **Corner kicks**  
  * There should be at least 5 yards between the opposing team and the player kicking the ball.

* **Goal kicks**  
  * The opposing team moves back up to the halfway line before the goal kick.  
  * This gives the kicking team a chance to pass and start moving up the field.  
  * Adjust as needed for fairness. If your team keeps intercepting before the other team can control the ball, pull them back farther.

* **No blowouts**  
  * While we don’t officially keep score, it’s obvious when one team dominates (e.g., 5-0).  
  * If this happens, the two head coaches should take a moment to discuss steps to keep the game fun for both teams. Possible solutions include:  
    * Encourage your team to focus on passing / playing keep away   
    * Let players who haven’t scored yet take the shots  
    * Move strong players to defense  
    * Reducing the dominant team’s players on the field

* **Referees and Timekeepers**  
  * There will be (1) Referee. Each team will need to provide center referees for 5 games in the Fall Season and 4 games in the Spring Season.   
  * Work together to ensure fair play and a positive environment.  
  * Set a good example for the kids by remaining respectful and constructive whenever there is a disagreement. The kids are watching, so demonstrate sportsmanship and teamwork\!  
  * All coaches and designated team referees will be required to take the 8U referee training course.  
  * Any team that does not have a referee show up to their assigned game or is unable to provide a referee; will be unable to have a goalkeeper for that match and the coaches will self-manage/referee the game.

\* Other considerations. If conflict or confusion, the visiting team does the first kickoff, home does kickoff for the second half. While each team generally sits on different sides, this is not a hard rule. To keep spectators and sitting players cool in the heat, sometimes both teams sit on the same side. Ideally coaches and parents can work together without needing Rules.

---

### **Coaching on Game Day**

* Stay on the sideline to coach during games, not behind the goals. Avoid running onto the field.  
* Prioritize teaching and encouragement over criticism.  
* Remember: It’s about **fun, development, and teamwork.**
