Coach's Sideline — audit against the Region 630 U8 guide 4 August 2026
Audit · second pass

Where the app and the guide disagree

Sixteen findings, ranked. Read against Guide for Coaching U8 at AYSO Region 630 (Fall 2026) and the app at master: index.html, app.js, app.css, lineup-core.js, drills.js.

Verdict

The rotation engine is no longer the risk. Every severity-1 item from the first pass is closed, the fairness ledger now runs on minutes actually played, and the keeper cap the guide sets is enforced rather than punted. What is left is concentrated in one place.

The weakest part of the app is the whistle. The guide permits substitutions only at designated breaks — and the break is the moment Game Day handles worst. Subs made there are applied to the period that just ended, the breaks themselves are untimed and missing from the "ends ≈" estimate, and tapping Start instead of Period + quietly re-runs the quarter that just finished.

Second: the guide's two countable duties — five centre referees in Fall, and the no-referee-no-keeper penalty — are printed in the Rules tab and tracked nowhere. Third, by area rather than by bug: "Communicate often with parents" is the guide's first reminder, and the app has no parent-facing surface at all, by construction. The only share link grants edit rights to the roster.

The clock and the break

Rank numbers are global across all sixteen findings.

01

Subs made at the break edit the period that just ended

Tap-to-sub always targets the period on the clock. During the break — whistle blown, coach at the bench, kids swapping — that is the quarter that just finished. The swap rewrites history, is credited the time left on the clock (at 0:00, nothing), and the quarter about to start is untouched. The coach walks away believing the change is made; the sheet sends the original child back on.

app.js · curPi() → min(period−1, Q−1) · subNow() · only Period + advances the period

Fix: when the clock is at 0:00, target the next period. Better: a "this period / next period" toggle on the On-the-field card, so the coach chooses what they are editing.

02

Start, after time expires, re-runs the same quarter

When the clock hits zero the button reverts to Start, and pressing it refills the period without advancing it. One plausible mis-tap gives you a twenty-minute quarter with the rotation frozen — six kids playing double, two sitting double, and a printed sheet describing a game that did not happen.

app.js · toggleTimer() → if(g.secs<=0) g.secs = minsper*60

Fix: after expiry the primary button becomes "Start period 3" and does both. Keep Period + for the coach who ends a quarter early.

07

"ends ≈" leaves out every break the guide mandates

The estimate sums remaining playing time only. A U8 game carries two sub breaks of 2–3 minutes and a halftime of 5, or 10 on a hot day, so the number on the scoreboard runs ten to sixteen minutes early. It is also the number a coach reads out to the parent asking when to come back.

app.js · renderEnds() → secs + (maxPeriods−period) × minsper × 60

Fix: add the guide's break lengths to the sum. They are fixed numbers; the estimate becomes honest for free.

08

The breaks are never timed

Period + toasts "Halftime — 5 min break (10 on a hot day)" and then times nothing, in an app whose centrepiece is a clock. The guide opens Game Format with starting at the scheduled time; the break is where a U8 game loses it.

Fix: Period + starts a break countdown — 2:30 between quarters, 5:00 at half, a hot-day switch for 10:00 — then arms the next period. This is also where finding 01 resolves naturally: the break is a state, so subs made in it have somewhere correct to land.

09

No scheduled kickoff time

Nothing holds when the game is meant to start, so nothing can say "kickoff in 12 minutes — start the warm-up." The guide asks for both: start on time, warm up beforehand. The checklist's warm-up line has nothing behind it, and the Warmup-tagged drills live only in Practice.

Fix: one time field per game. It pays for itself three ways — the warm-up prompt, an accurate "ends ≈", and a real timestamp on the archive row instead of the moment someone tapped Start.

Duties the guide counts

03

The referee obligation is the one number the guide holds you to, and nothing counts it

Five centre referees in Fall, four in Spring, per team — plus 8U referee training for every coach and team referee. The app's entire coverage is a sentence in Rules and an inert checkbox. This is the only requirement in the guide that is numeric, runs across the season, and carries a penalty.

Fix: the archive already writes one row per game. A "who reffed for us" field on that row gives "3 of 5 covered" on the season log, which is the whole feature.

04

"No referee means no goalkeeper" has no switch

The penalty is stated twice in the UI, and the machinery exists — the BU5 preset already builds keeperless sheets. But the only route to it is that preset, which also drops the field to four, relabels the header and swaps the whole rules card. The coach's real options on a ref-less Saturday are to misconfigure the app as a BU5 team or to play with a keeper they are not allowed.

app.js · FORMATS.bu5.keeper = false · lu.keeper is already per-lineup

Fix: a "no keeper this game" checkbox next to Build lineup. The sheet then prints without a GK column and the keeper ledger is left alone.

Sideline controls

05

The buttons pressed most often are the smallest ones on the screen

The score − and + controls measure 30×26 px: roughly 40% of the 44 px touch minimum, on a dark scoreboard, one-handed, while watching six seven-year-olds. Start, Period + and Reset — pressed four to six times a game — are full-width. The priority is inverted.

app.css · .score .sc-btns button {width:30px;height:26px} · .gd-controls button {flex:1}

Fix: 44 px minimum on the score steppers, and take the space from the clock, which is already the largest element on the page.

06

Reset has no stated scope, no confirmation, and it feeds the fairness math

It resets the clock only, but it sits beside Period + where it reads as "reset the game". And because a mid-period sub is credited the fraction of the period still on the clock, refilling the clock and then subbing credits the incoming player a full period and takes a full one off the outgoing player. A single unconfirmed tap corrupts the season ledger the rest of the app exists to protect.

app.js · resetTimer() · subNow() frac = game.secs / (minsper*60)

Fix: label it "Reset clock", and clamp the sub credit to the time the period has actually run rather than the time showing.

10

The pre-kickoff checklist sits on the planning tab and forgets everything

Nine checkboxes — shin guards, jewellery off, referee present, parents briefed — under Roster & Lineup, with no handler behind them, no persistence and no per-game reset. Saturday-morning work parked in the tab you open on a Wednesday, and Game Day never shows whether the walk down the line happened.

Fix: move it to the top of Game Day, store the ticks against the game id, and clear them when a new lineup is built. "2 of 9 done" belongs on the scoreboard card before kickoff.

16

The period-end alert probably never sounds on an iPhone

The beep builds a fresh AudioContext at the moment the clock expires — outside any user gesture, which is exactly the case iOS Safari leaves suspended. The app is built to be added to a phone's home screen, and this is the one signal that matters when nobody is looking at the screen. The visual alarm, by contrast, is good.

app.js · beep() → new AudioContext() inside tick()

Fix: create and unlock one context on the Start tap and reuse it. Worth verifying on a real handset either way.

Everything outside the coach's phone

11

Parents are the guide's first reminder, and the app cannot address them

"Communicate often with parents/guardians" and the pre-season meeting are reminders one and two. The app has no contact field, nothing to send, and no read-only view — and it cannot have one today, because the team token is an edit capability: share the link and you hand a parent the roster. The guide also carries two live links, the Coach-Spectator Behavior Guidelines and the practice guide. The app paraphrases both and links neither.

Fix: the two links today, in Rules and Practice — a five-minute change. The parent surface is a second read-only token on the team row, and it is the largest item in this audit.

12

The blowout nudge offers one of the guide's four remedies — the most disruptive one

The guide lists four: play keep-away, let the kids who haven't scored take the shots, move strong players to defence, reduce numbers on the field. The app surfaces only the last, and asks the coach to leave Game Day, change a dropdown on the Roster tab and rebuild — mid-game, at 5–0. Two of the other three are already features: "move strong players to defence" is precisely the D/F tap-swap two cards below the nudge. The guide also frames this as a conversation between the two head coaches; the nudge frames it as a unilateral setting change.

Fix: list all four in the nudge, wire the two the app can already do, and put "drop to 5 on the field" in the nudge itself instead of describing where the control lives.

13

Goals have no scorer, so the one data-driven remedy is impossible

A goal event records a side and a delta, nothing more. The season log reads "Goal — us", and "let players who haven't scored yet take the shots" cannot be answered by an app that has every other number about every child. The guide is clear that score is not officially kept, so attribution should be optional — but a coach is asked who scored by every parent at pickup.

app.js · logEvent("goal", {side, d}) · player_id left null

Fix: after tapping +, offer the six on-field chips as an optional "who?" — one tap, skippable, and the field is already in the event schema.

14

No opponent, no home or away

The archive's opponent column is hardcoded null and the far side is always labelled "Visitors". So the kickoff convention on the checklist — visitors take the first half, home the second — cannot be made specific, because the app does not know which you are. Season log rows read "8/4 · 3–2", with nobody on the other end.

Fix: an opponent field and a home/away toggle per game. The toggle turns an inert checklist line into "you kick off the second half".

15

Heat appears twice in the guide and once in the app

The guide raises it for halftime (extend to 10 minutes) and for seating (both teams on the shady side). The app has one checklist line and a parenthetical inside a toast. The 60-minute practice template — 8+8+6+9+8+9+12 — has no water break in it at all.

Fix: a hot-day switch on Game Day that lengthens the halftime countdown, and a 2-minute water item in the suggested plan.

Closed since the first pass (3 August)

Recorded because it changes where the risk now sits. The keeper dimension exists and the one-quarter cap is enforced, with a keeper swap that moves nobody off the field. Fairness runs across games on minutes actually played, not on the plan. Roster edits redraw only the unplayed part of the sheet instead of corrupting it. The false-positive "short on quarters" flag is now the comparative test the guide actually states. Mid-period safety subs exist and credit fractional periods. Non-standard formats warn on screen and on the printed sheet. The ticker is wall-clock anchored, and a synced device no longer shows a Pause button over a frozen clock. The library gained 4v4 Small-Sided, Keeper Hands and Throw-Ins, and the suggested plan no longer ends in a drill an eight-player team cannot run. The Rules tab, the pre-kickoff checklist and the blowout nudge are all new.

Still open from that pass: the guide's field dimensions, 12'×6' goals on a 50'×40' field, are printed verbatim with a caveat. Fifty feet is shorter than a six-yard box; it is almost certainly yards. Worth one email to Region 630.

Suggested order

  1. The break, as a state (01, 02, 08, and 07 with it). One feature: Period + runs a timed break, subs made during it land on the next period, and the primary button advances rather than repeating. This removes both remaining ways the sheet can describe a game that did not happen.
  2. Two control fixes (05, 06). Touch targets on the score steppers, and Reset relabelled and decoupled from the sub credit. An hour, and it protects the ledger.
  3. The two links, and the checklist moved to Game Day (11 in part, 10). Small, and both are things a coach touches every week.
  4. Referee ledger and the no-keeper switch (03, 04). One archive field and one checkbox cover the guide's only countable duty and its only penalty.
  5. Kickoff time, opponent, home/away (09, 14) — then the parent view (11), which needs a read-only token and is the one item here that is a project rather than a change.
