Team snacks

Pick a game to bring snacks for the team, and volunteer to referee a home game. Everyone with this link sees the same list, so you can see what's still open and who has the rest.

Volunteering to referee? Your team needs a certified 8U referee for its home games.

Signed up on this phone? You can change or give back your snack game or referee slot any time. Games come from the team's GameChanger schedule; home games are the ones that need a referee.

Become an 8U referee

Your team is expected to supply a certified 8U referee for its 5 home games this Fall season. The training is online at AYSO 630, in SportsConnect under AYSOU. Here's how to find it.

  1. Log into your account at ayso630.org.
  2. Click the AYSOU link on the left-hand side.
  3. Click Training Library.
  4. Click Referees.
  5. Select the 8U Official – Full Online Course (also offered in Spanish) and complete it.
  6. Don't forget to also complete the other required AYSO volunteer certifications.

Questions? Email CVPA@ayso630.org.
