      5Rules & conduct — BU5

      The short card for the BU5 side. ⚠ Region 630's BU5 specifics are not in the U8 guide — confirm periods, players on the field and period length before the first game.

      The format

      • No goalkeepers. Nobody handles the ball.
      • Small-sided, size 3 ball. Shin guards under the socks, no jewellery.
      • Everyone plays — the builder shares time evenly, same as U8.

      Coaching

      • They are four and five: fun first, one instruction at a time, water often.
      • Cheer, don't criticise. Parents: one voice, a yard back from the line.

      5Rules & conduct

      The reference half of the Region 630 U8 guide — the parts the app does not do for you. Nothing here has settings; it is here so you can check it on a Saturday morning.

      In play

      • Goalkeeper may not punt. Roll or throw it out, or place it down and play it with the feet.
      • Once the keeper places the ball on the ground, they may not pick it up again.
      • Hands only inside the box — outside it the keeper is an ordinary player.
      • Throw-in: both hands, ball starts behind the head, both feet stay on the ground. Coach the technique, don't just re-take it.
      • Corner kicks: opponents stay 5 yards back.
      • Goal kicks: opponents retreat to the halfway line.
      • Every player keeps for a quarter, maximum one quarter each.
      • No referee for the match ⇒ no goalkeeper for that match.

      Playing time

      • Everyone plays at least 3 of 4 quarters before anyone plays a 4th.
      • One player may sit 2 quarters in a game — rotate that responsibility across games. The lineup builder now tracks this for you.
      • Safety and injury substitutions are allowed at any time, and the player may return in the same quarter.

      Timing

      • Two 20-minute halves, played as four 10-minute quarters.
      • Substitution break mid-half: 2–3 minutes.
      • Halftime: 5 minutes, 10 on a hot day.
      • Start on time. Warm up before the start time, not into it.

      Coaching on game day

      • Stay on the sideline. Not behind the goals.
      • Do not run onto the field.
      • Teach; don't criticise. They are seven.
      • If one team is running away with it (say 5–0), reduce the dominant team's players on the field. Game Day will nudge you.

      Parents

      • Hold a pre-season meeting and share the AYSO behaviour guidelines.
      • Cheer, don't coach — the kids can only follow one voice.
      • Pick one sideline spot and stay about a yard back from the line.

      Referees

      • Each team supplies 5 centre referees in Fall, 4 in Spring.
      • 8U referee training is required for all coaches and team referees.

      Equipment

      • Shin guards, worn under the socks. Cleats. Size 3 ball.
      • No jewellery or watches — removed, not taped over.

      Field

      • 6v6 with keeper.
      • Goals 12 × 6, field 50 × 40, as written in the guide. The units are almost certainly yards — 50 feet would be shorter than a six-yard box. Worth confirming with Region 630.
